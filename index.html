What this site is — and is not

Public (this site): findings, risk analysis, remediation and re-testing results, architecture and report summary. Read-only documentation. No application runtime, no database, no credentials.
Private (not published): the intentionally vulnerable OWASP Juice Shop application and the live before/after laboratory. It runs only on an isolated local host, bound to loopback, and is deliberately unreachable from the internet. Controlled validation of the vulnerabilities requires that private environment.
